Privacy Policy

Shotdrop collects no personal information.

What the app reads

  • The screen’s content, only while capturing. Shots go only where you choose (clipboard, folder)

When the app goes online

  • Checking for updates — once a day it reads the list of versions on GitHub (github.com). Nothing that identifies you is sent. You can turn it off in Settings.

What is stored on your Mac

  • Settings such as shortcuts, in macOS preferences. They never leave your Mac.
  • Your license key and the date your trial started (or, if you used the earlier free version, a note saying so), in the macOS keychain. The license is checked on your Mac alone; nothing is sent.

When you buy

Payment is handled by a reseller. They process your card details; we never receive them, and their privacy policy applies. We only learn what is needed to issue your license (such as the email address the key is sent to).

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visits (such as IP addresses). We use no analytics, ads or cookies.
