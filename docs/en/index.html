What it does

Full screen ⌃⇧1

The whole screen the pointer is on, even with several displays.

Selection ⌃⇧2

Drag an area or click a window. Windows come with their shadow, even the parts hidden behind others.

Scrolling capture ⌃⇧3

Scrolls the area by itself and stitches it into one tall image. Sticky headers and sidebars appear once, and the page goes back to where it was.

Choose where it goes

Clipboard, folder, or both, switchable from the menu bar panel. PNG or JPEG.

Your shortcuts

Change all three keys. They work alongside macOS’s own ⌘⇧3 and ⌘⇧4.

Stays on your Mac

Screenshots never leave your Mac.

With other tsubomi apps

With other tsubomi apps such as Profilink and TouchRotator running, the menu bar icons can be combined into a single 🌸.
